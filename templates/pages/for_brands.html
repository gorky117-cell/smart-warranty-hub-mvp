For brands and service partners

Smart Warranty Hub stays with owners after the purchase. With their consent, it gives brands, insurers, retailers and service partners a clearer view of how products do after the sale.

Brands (OEMs)

  • Anonymous after-sale insight by model, region and product group (groups of 10+ owners, with consent).
  • Early risk signals and monthly trends in the brand portal.
  • Questions to owners and care suggestions sent through SWH, with consent and frequency limits.

Insurers & TPAs

  • Customers can share a ready claim pack: invoice, warranty facts, product details and guided-check results.
  • Guided diagnostics for products that aren't connected.

Retailers

  • Care tips and early warnings that help customers avoid avoidable failures.
  • Clear claim routes that send customers to the brand's service, not the store counter.

Suppliers & service partners

  • In pilot: issue-trend signals for parts-demand planning, and earlier alerts so critical parts are ready.

Start with a pilot

We start small: one product group, measured against your current numbers. Pilot goals are targets we will measure with our first partners. Measured results will be published after the pilots.
