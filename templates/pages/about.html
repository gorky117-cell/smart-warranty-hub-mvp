About Smart Warranty Hub

Smart Warranty Hub looks after the things you own after you buy them. You upload the invoice once. We read it, find the brand's warranty terms and show you, in plain words, how long the warranty lasts, what it covers and what to do if something goes wrong.

What we do for you

  • Warranty facts from the brand's own terms, written in our own words, with the source and the date we checked it.
  • When we are not sure about something, we say "please check" instead of guessing.
  • Reminders before a warranty ends and when care is due.
  • Care tips that fit how you use the product.
  • Help with the steps when you need to make a claim.

What we do for brands and partners

Brands, insurers, retailers and service partners can see how products do after the sale, as anonymous totals from owners who agreed to share. Read more for brands and partners.
