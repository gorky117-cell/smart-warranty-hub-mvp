Privacy

This page explains in plain words what we keep about you and why. We never sell personal data.

What we store

  • Your account: your user name, your email address and your password (stored only as a scrambled hash, never as written).
  • The invoices and files you upload, and the product details we read from them.
  • The answers you give us about your products, and the reminders you set.
  • Basic security records, such as when you signed in, so we can protect your account.

Why we use it

  • To show your warranty facts, care tips and reminders.
  • To send you emails you need: password reset links and reminders about your products.
  • To keep the service safe and working.

Who helps us

We use trusted companies to run the service: hosting for our servers and database, an email service to send messages, and text-reading and AI services that help us read invoices. They get only what they need for that job.

Brands and partners

Brands and partners see only anonymous totals, never your name or details. Anything more needs your permission, and you can change your choice anytime.

Your choices

  • To change your sharing choices, or to see, correct or delete your data, email support@smartwarrantyhub.com from the address on your account.

Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in and to protect forms. No advertising cookies.

This is a plain-language summary. Questions: support@smartwarrantyhub.com.
