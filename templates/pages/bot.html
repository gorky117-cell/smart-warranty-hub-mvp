Our web reader

To show customers correct warranty facts, Smart Warranty Hub sometimes reads a brand's public warranty, care and support pages. This page explains how our reader behaves.

How to recognise it

It identifies itself with a user agent that starts with SmartWarrantyHub, for example SmartWarrantyHub/1.0.

What it does

  • It reads your robots.txt first and follows its rules.
  • If robots.txt cannot be read (for example an error or a timeout), it does not read the site.
  • It reads only public pages. It never signs in, fills in forms or reads pages behind a log-in.
  • It keeps what it read for a while, so it does not need to come back often.
  • Customers see facts in our own words, with a link to your page as the source.

How to block it

Add these lines to your robots.txt:

User-agent: SmartWarrantyHub
Disallow: /

Questions, or want us to stop reading your site? Write to support@smartwarrantyhub.com.
