Stakeholder Deep View

Choose a stakeholder to see pain points, Smart Warranty Hub response, KPI targets, and operational outcomes.

User Pain Points

  • Warranty papers are lost or hard to understand.
  • Claim steps are unclear when product fails.
  • Users get no early warning before expiry or risk.

What SWH Does

  • Extracts invoice and warranty terms via OCR.
  • Shows simple summary, exclusions, and claim path.
  • Sends expiry and care nudges for preventive action.

User KPI Targets

Failure Prevention Rate>= 25%
High-risk users who acted and avoided failure in window.
Claim Turnaround-30%
Median claim close time improvement versus baseline.
False Alert Rate<= 20%
Lower false alarms to maintain user trust and retention.

OEM Pain Points

  • Late visibility into model-level field failures.
  • High warranty payout and avoidable service load.
  • Weak linkage between user behavior and product outcomes.

What SWH Does

  • Builds early warning signals from claims and behavior.
  • Forecasts risk trends for monthly planning.
  • Routes actionable intelligence via OEM dashboard flows.

OEM KPI Targets

Early Warning Lead Time>= 14 days
Time gained before expected failure spikes.
High-Risk Precision>= 55%
Signal quality to reduce alert fatigue in operations.
Warranty Cost / Unit-10% to -15%
Direct financial impact from proactive intervention.

TPA Pain Points

  • High manual effort in triage and adjudication queues.
  • Re-opened cases from inconsistent decisions.
  • Fraud checks consume significant analyst bandwidth.

What SWH Does

  • Pre-classifies claims and highlights needed evidence.
  • Supports guided diagnostics and structured intake.
  • Flags suspicious patterns for focused human review.

TPA KPI Targets

Auto-Triage Rate>= 50%
Claims automatically classified before manual review.
Adjudication TAT-25%
Faster decision cycle with workflow assistance.
Re-open Rate<= 8%
Decision quality signal for stable claim closure.

Retailer Pain Points

  • Returns and escalations increase support costs.
  • Low post-sale visibility into product care and usage.
  • Poor coordination with OEM support teams.

What SWH Does

  • Improves return prevention through user nudges.
  • Shares aggregate product health insights with OEM workflows.
  • Improves escalation routing and service transparency.

Retailer KPI Targets

Return / Repair Rate-10%
Reduced 30/60/90-day return and repair pressure.
Escalations / 1,000 Units-20%
Lower support burden and better customer experience.

Supplier Pain Points

  • Demand uncertainty causes stockouts or overstock.
  • No proactive signal when field issues rise.
  • Slow response to critical parts demand spikes.

What SWH Does

  • Provides trend signals tied to issue and forecast layers.
  • Helps align critical inventory with predicted demand.
  • Improves service SLA readiness across partner network.

End-to-End Operating Flow

One platform from ingestion to action, designed for simple navigation and stakeholder visibility.

1

Ingest and Extract

Capture invoice or warranty input. OCR and parsing standardize brand, model, serial, and date signals.

2

Normalize Terms

Coverage, exclusions, claim steps, and source confidence are harmonized for user-ready summaries.

3

Score and Forecast

Behavior, telemetry, and incident patterns drive risk score layers and monthly forecasting views.

4

Trigger Action

Nudges, alerts, diagnostics routes, and service workflows move each case toward prevention and faster closure.

5

Measure KPI Outcomes

Platform reports KPI movement for users, OEMs, TPAs, retailers, and suppliers in one traceable layer.
