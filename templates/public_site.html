Who we help

Pain points

  • Warranty details are hard to find and understand.
  • Claim steps are confusing when something breaks.
  • No warning before a problem or before the warranty ends.

How SWH helps

  • Reads your invoice and finds the brand's warranty facts, with source and date.
  • Shows what's covered, what isn't and how to claim, in plain words.
  • Reminds you before expiry and when care is due.
  • Care tips based on how you use the product and where you live.
  • Stays with you after purchase, not only at claim time.
Pilot goal
  • At least 25% fewer avoidable breakdowns among owners who follow SWH tips, compared with owners who don't.
  • Claims closed 30% faster than the partner's current average.
  • No more than 1 in 5 alerts turns out to be unnecessary.

Pain points

  • Product problems are seen late.
  • High warranty and service costs.
  • Little visibility by model and region after sale, especially for products sold through retailers.

How SWH helps

  • Anonymous after-sale insight by model, region and product group (groups of 10+ owners, with consent).
  • Early risk signals and monthly trends in the brand portal.
  • Questions to owners and care suggestions sent through SWH, with consent and frequency limits.
  • When connected: EV battery and smart-device signals, with the owner's permission.
Pilot goal
  • Issue signals at least 14 days before a service spike.
  • At least 55% of high-risk flags confirmed by a real service event.
  • 10-15% lower warranty cost per unit in pilot product groups.

  • What we store: your invoice, the product details we read from it, and the answers you give us. Nothing more than we need to look after your products.
