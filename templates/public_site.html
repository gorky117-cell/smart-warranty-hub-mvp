User Pain Points

  • Warranty details are hard to find and understand.
  • Claim steps are confusing during failure.
  • No timely warning before risk or expiry.

What SWH Does

  • Reads invoice/warranty via OCR.
  • Shows clear summary, exclusions, and claim path.
  • Sends behavior and expiry nudges to reduce failures.

User KPI Targets

Failure Prevention Rate>= 25%
Users who acted on nudges and avoided failure.
Claim Turnaround-30%
Faster claim closure compared to baseline.
False Alert Rate<= 20%
Keeps alerts useful and trusted.

OEM Pain Points

  • Late warning of product failures.
  • High warranty and service costs.
  • Limited field visibility by model/region.

What SWH Does

  • Builds early warning signals.
  • Shows monthly risk and forecast trends.
  • Supports OEM actions in dashboard workflow.
  • EV battery health and IoT insights activate when telemetry is connected.

OEM KPI Targets

Early Warning Lead Time>= 14 days
How early OEM can act before spikes.
High-Risk Precision>= 55%
Better signal quality, less noise.
Warranty Cost / Unit-10% to -15%
Direct cost impact from proactive action.

TPA Pain Points

  • High manual triage effort.
  • Case reopens due to inconsistent decisions.
  • Fraud checks are time-consuming.

What SWH Does

  • Pre-classifies claims with needed evidence.
  • Supports guided diagnostics for non-IoT devices.
  • Flags suspicious patterns for review.

TPA KPI Targets

Auto-Triage Rate>= 50%
More claims triaged automatically.
Adjudication TAT-25%
Faster claim decisions.
Re-open Rate<= 8%
Better closure quality.
