Two-Minute Live Flow

  1. Open the public site.
    Position it as a customer, OEM and TPA warranty operating layer.
  2. Sign in and open Neo dashboard.
    Use the prepared demo account and avoid live dependency surprises.
  3. Upload or select a sample invoice.
    Show product identity, purchase date, coverage and evidence context.
  4. Open the warranty summary.
    Highlight coverage, exclusions, claim steps, confidence and source traceability.
  5. Show care and risk guidance.
    Explain predictive and behaviour signals as support guidance, not automated claim decisions.
  6. Open the Resolution checklist.
    State clearly that the agent is draft-only and cannot submit claims or contact OEMs.
  7. Switch to OEM dashboard.
    Show aggregate insight, controlled source verification, question studio and recommendation studio.
  8. Close with privacy boundaries.
    Direct OEM sharing requires explicit consent; aggregate insight is cohort-suppressed.

Proof Points To Show

  • Invoice upload has size/type restrictions and server-generated filenames.
  • Warranty data access is protected by authenticated ownership checks.
  • OpenAI/LLM capability is optional and deterministic fallbacks keep the core flow usable.
  • Telemetry strips direct identifiers and OEM aggregate insight uses cohort suppression.
  • High-risk and high-cost routes have CSRF protection, rate limits, request IDs and per-user AI quota gates.
  • The warranty-resolution agent is draft-only and records an audit trace.

Q&A Answers

Is this production-ready?

It is ready for a controlled hackathon demo and pilot. Full production rollout would move local/process-bound stores to managed Postgres, object storage and shared rate-limit/quota infrastructure.

What does AI decide?

AI enriches and explains information. Deterministic warranty data, policy checks, consent, ownership and human review boundaries remain authoritative.

How is customer data protected?

Users are isolated by ownership checks, telemetry is sanitized, aggregate OEM insight is cohort-suppressed and direct OEM communication requires explicit consent.

What is the business value?

Customers get clearer coverage and earlier care guidance. OEM and TPA teams get privacy-safe early signals for support, service and quality workflows.
