Synthetic Acmeco ZX-100 Warranty Terms

Test fixture only. This is not an official OEM document.

Coverage

Standard warranty coverage is 12 months from purchase date.

  • Base parts and labour are covered within the stated warranty period.
  • Firmware support updates are included where applicable.
  • Manufacturing defects under normal household use are covered.

Exclusions

  • Physical or liquid damage is not covered.
  • Unauthorised repair or tampering voids coverage.
  • Improper installation or maintenance outside guidance is excluded.

Claim steps

  1. Keep invoice or receipt ready.
  2. Share serial/model information with support.
  3. Provide error evidence such as photos or logs for faster triage.
