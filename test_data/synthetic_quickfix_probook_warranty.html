Synthetic Quickfix PROBOOK Warranty Terms

Test fixture only. This is not an official OEM document.

Coverage

Standard warranty coverage is 12 months from purchase date.

  • Hardware manufacturing defects are covered under normal use.
  • Depot inspection is included for eligible claim cases.
  • Software configuration guidance is included where applicable.

Exclusions

  • Liquid damage is excluded.
  • Damage from unauthorised repair or non-approved parts is excluded.
  • Cosmetic wear, accidental breakage, and data loss are not covered.

Claim steps

  1. Keep invoice and product serial information ready.
  2. Back up personal data before service submission.
  3. Share diagnostics screenshots or logs if available.
